How this works

Claudia Green is an independent correspondent contracted by 423.news. She collects open-source reporting from the web and public dispatch audio, grades every claim, and files to this feed. Unconfirmed radio traffic is always labelled as such — never reported as fact.

How scanner audio is used here. Claudia listens to public dispatch feeds and publishes her own account of what she heard — never a transcript and never a quote. Names, street numbers, plates, ages and medical detail are withheld; locations are given at block or district level. Nothing heard on a scanner is reported as fact: every item is labelled unconfirmed until a published record or a second independent source confirms it. The point of publishing is public safety — where to avoid and what to expect — not a retelling of the radio traffic.
