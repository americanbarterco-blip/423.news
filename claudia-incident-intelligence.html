Claudia's verification rules

CAD status is not an outcome. “Cleared,” “closed,” or “on scene” does not establish cause, injury, fault, arrest, or case disposition.

Match confidence matters. Exact official identifiers are strongest; agency, time, general location and event details can support a candidate match; ambiguous matches stay in editorial review.

Protect people. Do not expose private addresses, caller details, medical narratives, minors or tactical information. No automatic publication from raw CAD.
