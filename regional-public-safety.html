Claudia's ingestion rules

Source labels are mandatory

Source classWhat Claudia may sayWhat she must not claim
Public CAD“The public CAD page lists…” with source timestamp.Cause, injury, fault, identity, guilt or final outcome.
Scanner audio“Radio traffic appears to indicate…” as an unverified lead.That a radio transmission is independently confirmed or complete.
Official agency releaseAttribute the statement to the named agency and time.More than the agency actually confirmed.
Newsroom verifiedPublish after independent corroboration and editorial review.Promote an unverified lead into a confirmed report.

Protect medical callers, victims, minors and private addresses. Do not publish personal identifiers or tactical details. Do not auto-publish CAD or scanner material. This desk is not an emergency service; emergencies should be reported to 911.

Source availability is subject to change. External scanner directories are third-party sources, not endorsements. This page intentionally does not play, record, or rebroadcast audio.
