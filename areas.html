Our expanded Southeast Tennessee focus includes Chattanooga and Hamilton County, Cleveland and Bradley County, Athens and McMinn County, Dayton and Rhea County, Polk County communities such as Benton, Copperhill and Ducktown, and Grundy County communities such as Tracy City, Altamont and Coalmont. The 423 and 729 area codes share the same two non-contiguous regions in eastern Tennessee.

Coverage note: Telephone area-code boundaries follow rate-center and exchange boundaries, not county lines. These counties are served in whole or in part; this directory is a way to find local coverage, not a claim that every address or community has identical service or reporting availability.
