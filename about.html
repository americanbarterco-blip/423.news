ABOUT THE NEWSROOM

About 423 News

423 News (423.news) is an independent digital local news publication focused on Chattanooga, Cleveland, Bradley County and communities across Tennessee's 423 region, with coverage extending into nearby North Georgia.

Our coverage brings together local news, public safety, traffic, weather, schools, high-school and community sports, local government, outdoors, events and neighborhood information.

Local coverage, clearly identified

We aim to make useful regional information easier to find in one place. Reports should identify their source and distinguish confirmed facts from developing or unverified details. Scanner activity and community submissions are leads for verification, not proof by themselves.

Community participation

Residents can share news tips, photos, events and community updates through citizen423report. Submissions may require verification and moderation before publication.
