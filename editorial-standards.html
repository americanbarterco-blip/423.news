423 NEWS · PUBLICATION POLICY

Editorial Standards & Corrections

These standards explain how 423 News approaches local reporting across Chattanooga, Cleveland, Bradley County and the wider 423 region. They are intended to guide publication decisions and reader expectations.

Accuracy and verification

We seek to verify material facts before presenting them as established. We prioritize original records, direct statements from responsible agencies or organizations, on-the-record sources and corroboration. A single social post, anonymous tip, scanner transmission or unconfirmed message is a lead—not proof. When facts are incomplete, reports should say what is known, what is not known and when information was last checked.

Developing news and public safety

Newsworthiness is not limited by topic. Crime, violence, fatal accidents, missing persons, emergencies, courts, public safety, government, schools, sports, outdoors and community events may all warrant coverage. We do not treat an entire subject area as automatically off limits. We apply stronger verification, careful wording and privacy safeguards when the risks of error or harm are higher.

We avoid publishing unsupported allegations, speculation about cause or fault, unnecessary identifying details about victims or minors, and details that could compromise an active response. A person's inclusion in a public record or online post does not by itself make every personal detail appropriate to republish.

Sources, attribution and AI assistance

Reports should identify the source of important claims where practical and distinguish firsthand reporting, public records, official statements, community submissions and other material. AI tools may assist with organization, transcription, translation or drafting, but generated text is not a source and does not replace human verification. Automated monitoring, including scanner or social feeds, can surface leads but cannot independently establish that an event occurred.

Corrections and updates

When we learn that a published report contains a material error, we aim to correct it promptly and make the correction clear to readers. Significant updates should be identified as updates rather than silently changing the meaning of an earlier report. Readers can submit a correction request or supporting documentation through the community page; include the article link, the specific issue and reliable supporting information.

Privacy, minors and sensitive information

We consider public interest, relevance, accuracy and potential harm before publishing personal information. We take additional care with minors, victims, people who are not public figures, private addresses, medical details and people who may be at risk. We do not assume that information being technically public means it is necessary to publish.

Community submissions

Tips, photos, videos and event listings may be reviewed for authenticity, context, rights and safety. Submission does not guarantee publication. We may request original files, dates, locations or additional confirmation. We do not knowingly present an unverified submission as independently confirmed reporting.

Advertising and sponsored material

Advertising, sponsorships and paid placements should be distinguishable from editorial reporting. Payment or a business relationship should not determine the conclusions of a news report. Sponsored or promotional material should be labeled clearly when published.

Contact and accountability

For corrections, source documents or questions about a report, use the site's community submission route and identify the relevant story. We will review the information and make changes when warranted. See About 423 News for publication and coverage information.

These standards may be updated as the publication's processes develop. This page describes editorial expectations; it is not a claim that every story is error-free.
