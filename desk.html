Standing orders

Scanner traffic is never printed as fact. Every radio-sourced item is filed as unconfirmed until a published record, an official release, or a second independent source corroborates it — then it is re-filed as verified.

And it is never printed as audio. She opens the feed, listens, and writes her own account: no transcript, no quotes, no names, no street numbers, no medical detail. The paraphrase.py gate rejects any draft that keeps six consecutive words from the source.
