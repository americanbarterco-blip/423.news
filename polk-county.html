Polk County, Tennessee — a core 423 News coverage area

Polk County deserves consistent, countywide attention. 423 News is building a dedicated local coverage hub for Benton, Copperhill, Ducktown, the Ocoee area and rural communities throughout the county—not just occasional stories when a major event happens.

Our coverage commitment: Polk County is a priority county, not a secondary outlying market. We aim to build regular reporting relationships and a dependable stream of useful local information across the county's communities. We will not claim coverage or staffing we do not yet have.

What we cover

Local government and public meetings; schools and student activities; public safety and courts; roads, traffic and weather; local business and jobs; agriculture and outdoors; sports; community events; and useful neighborhood information. Coverage is topic-neutral and based on newsworthiness, available evidence and public interest.

Accuracy matters. Scanner traffic, social posts and reader tips are leads—not proof. Developing information is labeled as such; we distinguish confirmed facts from what remains unknown.
