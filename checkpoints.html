Checkpoints, Accidents & Roadblocks

Publicly announced DUI/sobriety checkpoints and roadblocks, plus live accidents and road closures in the 423 area. Checkpoint entries expire automatically. Always follow posted signs and officer instructions.

Driving alerts use your phone's location and direction of travel. Your location stays on your device and is never sent or stored. Don't operate your phone while driving: set up the alerts before you go, or have a passenger use it. Turn on Voice to hear alerts hands-free.

Plan a safe ride. If you're drinking, use a designated driver, taxi, or rideshare. Information is provided for public awareness, may be delayed or incomplete, and may change without notice. Accident data comes from TomTom live traffic and can lag by several minutes.
