Checkpoints & Roadblocks

Publicly announced DUI/sobriety checkpoints and roadblocks in the 423 area. Entries expire automatically. Always follow posted signs and officer instructions.

Driving alerts use your phone's location and direction of travel. Your location stays on your device and is never sent or stored. Don't operate your phone while driving: set up the alerts before you go, or have a passenger use it.

Plan a safe ride. If you're drinking, use a designated driver, taxi, or rideshare. Checkpoint info is provided for public awareness and may change without notice.
